Take a step back and review the overall objective of the PR before the details:

- Does the PR's objective make sense, and does the description match what the code
  change actually does?
- Are there alternative ways to meet the same objective that would be simpler? Lay
  out the trade-offs (pros and cons) so the author can weigh them.

Say so early if the goal is unclear, the description drifts from the code, or a
simpler approach exists.
